# Paper for funding approval (format exercise): used-water sewers to serve Tengah

*Personal desk study, not a PUB paper or a PUB plan. The sewer layout is synthetic and most rates and durations are assumed; see the README. Generated from `results/` by `scripts/run_all.py`. Prices: 1Q2026.*

## 1. Aim

To seek approval in principle for a programme of gravity sewers to serve the Tengah planning area, at an estimated S$ 164 million (range S$ 98 to 269 million), and to note the expenditure of S$ 43.4 million falling in the 5-year block FY2027 to FY2031.

## 2. Background

Tengah is planned for about 42,000 homes (HDB). At the design basis of PUB's Code of Practice on Sewerage and Sanitary Works (3.5 persons per dwelling, 230 and 250 litres per capita per day for public and private housing, peak factor 3), and with proxy rates for non-residential land, the town generates an average flow of 39.9 ML/d and a design peak of 1,384 L/s. Flows were built up parcel by parcel from the URA Master Plan 2019.

## 3. Proposal

A network of 21.8 km of gravity sewers, 200 to 1350 mm, along the town's roads to one outlet, in 5 packages matched to the order in which the subzones are occupied. Every reach meets the Code's depth-of-flow limit; 12 short head reaches do not reach the minimum velocity and need a layout change at design stage. On flat ground the network reaches 21.5 m deep, so 8.6 km is assumed pipe-jacked.

## 6. Risks

- **Development timing.** If occupation comes one year earlier, the block needs S$ 83.9 million and 1 package(s) cannot be ready in time; one year later, S$ 5.8 million. The block should be reviewed whenever HDB's programme moves.
- **Downstream capacity.** The ultimate peak needs at least an empty 1500 mm receiving sewer (2100 mm if half its capacity is in use). A smaller or fuller sewer adds a downstream expansion that is not in this estimate.
- **Depth and ground.** The flat-ground assumption sets the depth; real levels could cut or add to the jacked length.
- **Industrial flows.** The industrial estate's flow is a placeholder.

## 7. Recommendation

Start scheme planning and agency consultation now: they cost little, they are on the critical path, and with the assumed lead times the first package has only 6 months of float. Seek approval in principle for the programme, and return with a firm estimate once the points below are settled.

## 8. What a real study would need before this paper could go forward

PUB's network model and the actual connection point and downstream capacity; the existing sewers already serving the town; ground and platform levels, utilities and soil information; the development programme by parcel from HDB and URA; design flows for the industrial estate; PUB's own cost norms from tendered rates; and the actual approval calendar.
